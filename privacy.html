Privacy

Last updated 1 October 2026

Your decks and matches

Without an account, everything you enter (decks, matches, sideboard guides, screenshots and replay files) stays in your own browser, in local storage and IndexedDB. Nothing is sent to us. Clearing your browser's site data deletes it, so export a backup from the Data tab.

If you create an account, your username, your password (stored hashed) and your prep data are kept in a Supabase database so you can use them on other devices. Each account can only read its own data. Signing out removes the copy in that browser. To have an account and its data deleted, open an issue on the GitHub repository.

Advertising

The site may show ads from Google AdSense to pay for further development. Google and its partners use cookies to serve ads based on your visits to this and other websites. You can turn off personalised ads in Google's My Ad Center, and read how Google uses information from sites that use its services. Visitors in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland are asked for consent before personalised ads are shown.

Other services

  • The page is hosted on GitHub Pages, which keeps standard server logs.
  • Fonts load from Google Fonts, and card data comes from Riftcodex.

Contact

Questions or requests: open an issue on the GitHub repository.
